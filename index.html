a quick manifesto for my anti-lgbtq+ laws database Oct 21st

LGBTQIA+ people, across the world, are facing unprecedented violence in every facet of life. 


Political violence is one of them. Homosexuality remains criminalized in over 50 countries, and the United States has taken abhorrent, consequential steps to snuff us out. 


In such times, it's incredibly difficult, and especially unrealistic to expect our communities to maintain hope. With this dataset, I hope to provide LGBTQIA+ researchers with a more accessible pathway to researching the ways in which language and policy harm our community. 


I feel as though I don't use my platforms to make abundantly clear that homophobia, transphobia, and queerphobia are absolute abominations of society- to be clear, I find it my life's mission to eradicate bigotry. Research may not be the best way to do so, but that lofty goal remains regardless.


– Love Adu, former GLSEN (now GLISTEN) National Student Council member, former Campus Advisory Committee on the LGBTQ+ Communities at Cal member, former OutVote Research Fellow, founding Niji-zu Advisory Council member, friendly-neighborhood academic lesbian.
